Summary

Vinicius do Prado Capanema holds a Bachelor's degree in Forest Engineering, a specialization in Rural Property Georeferencing accredited by INCRA, a Master's and a Ph.D. in Remote Sensing, and an MBA in Project Management. With a multidisciplinary background, he operates in an integrated manner across research, development, and innovation in the road infrastructure sector. He has extensive experience in conceiving, building, and deploying technology-driven solutions that leverage geoprocessing, geostatistics, and remote sensing for data analysis and manipulation, with emphasis on cloud computing, geographic databases, and spatial data infrastructure (SDI).

Consolidated experience in applying Artificial Intelligence to geospatial data management and analysis, with focus on process automation, operational efficiency improvement, and strategic decision support. Works with generative AI as a catalyst for technical productivity, integrating advanced models into geoprocessing routines, remote sensing, data governance, and spatial data engineering. Strong capability in structuring intelligent workflows, optimizing pipelines, qualifying information, standardizing procedures, and enhancing analytical and documentary production. Leverages AI as an executive tool to elevate delivery quality, reduce response time, support complex decision-making, and drive innovation in multidisciplinary, data-driven environments.

By applying advanced geoprocessing techniques to remote sensing-derived data, he conducts studies focused on monitoring and mapping immediate and adjacent factors that may impact road infrastructure development and management, providing decision support for strategic planning.

Beyond technical research, he contributes to multidisciplinary projects involving engineering, the environment, and technology, fostering the integration of interdisciplinary teams to ensure effective project delivery. His expertise in forest engineering and remote sensing enables him to perform environmental impact analyses, develop computational models, and create decision-support tools, with integration into cloud architectures and corporate spatial data ecosystems, contributing to project sustainability.

Alongside operational activities, he is dedicated to knowledge dissemination through the preparation and publication of scientific studies, technical reports, and active participation in conferences, seminars, and training sessions, strengthening best practices and encouraging the continuous improvement of applied methodologies.

About the Portfolio

Below you will find a selection of our deployed web-based solutions, including the integrated ecosystem of the PLI Hub. All applications are conceived under an open-source theoretical and conceptual framework, leveraging open tools and well-established standards tailored to each specific challenge. We operate across domains such as multi-criteria analysis (AHP/Saaty), extraction and processing of geospatial data (OpenStreetMap, DEM, Shapefiles, GeoTIFFs) with cloud computing (Google Earth Engine), WebGIS (Leaflet/OpenLayers, QGIS Server, GeoServer, PostGIS), and logistics/territorial and infrastructure planning — always with reproducible pipelines (Python/GeoPandas/Rasterio, ETL, Git/Docker version control) and integration with systems such as SIGMA-PLI and DER/SP.

Reinforcing our commitment to science, we combine scientific principles — modeling, inference, and data governance/quality — with technology — modern architectures, interoperability, and automation — to deliver transparent, auditable, and scalable solutions. This rigorous association between theory and tooling enhances the reliability of our analyses and prepares our products for large volumes and diverse datasets, meeting a wide range of demands.

Production Solutions

SIGMA — AHP Calculator 2.0

Multi-criteria decision-support tool based on Saaty's Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), with a guided five-step workflow: criteria selection and naming, pairwise comparisons, automatic weight calculation, and consistency ratio (CR) validation. SIGMA-PLI interface with interactive visualizations, manual or matrix upload input, and export to PDF, CSV, and XLSX.

AHP/Saaty SIGMA-PLI Multi-Criteria Analysis JavaScript

Indicator Adherence Calculator 2.0

PLI SIGMA system in three stages for methodological indicator assessment: minimum data sheet assembly (manual or CSV), scoring with descriptive rubrics, weights, and knockout criteria, and consolidated results with sensitivity analysis and exports. Evaluates seven analytical dimensions — conceptual proximity, causal mechanism, empirical validity, specificity, sensitivity, measurability, and cost-effectiveness.

Indicators CSV Upload Rubrics PLI SIGMA
Launch Application

Road Jurisdictionalization DER/SP

Methodological decision-support tool for the jurisdictionalization of road elements under DER/SP. Analyzes 1,247 roads using AHP with environmental, socioeconomic, and territorial integration criteria. Generates spatial favorability (IFM), socioeconomic (IFS), and infrastructure quality (IQI) indicators.

AHP Geoprocessing Infrastructure DER/SP
